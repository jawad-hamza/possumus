FAQ

A few questions buyers usually ask first.

Does every plan start with the same free setup period?

Yes. Each package starts with 3 free days, and the owner unlocks them by creating the account, verifying the email, and finishing business setup. Paid plans can be chosen or upgraded later.

Can {{ checkout_provider }} be connected later?

Yes. The pricing cards are already structured for plan-specific checkout links, so we only need to plug in the {{ checkout_provider }} URLs you share.

Will owners control the whole workspace?

Yes. The signed-up owner account becomes the admin with full control, while cashier or staff access can stay limited by role.

Can this evolve into a full SaaS?

Yes. This landing page and pricing flow are a public-facing first step before we finish the multi-tenant onboarding and subscription architecture.

Ready to package this as a real SaaS front door?

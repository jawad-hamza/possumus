Set up for your shop, whatever it sells

No country or trade is assumed. The wording, the units and the money are settings.

Weights and units
Sell by piece, box, pack or dozen, and by weight, volume or length. Quantities carry three decimals, so half a kilo of flour is a line like any other.
Pricing on cost
Type a sale price, or set a markup on cost as a percentage or as a fixed amount. A minimum margin is enforced either way, and cost is a weighted average of your purchases.
Sizes of one item
Small, medium and large sit under one product name. Each size keeps its own code, price, cost and stock, and the till asks which size.
Currency and tax
The currency symbol, the tax label and the tax rates are yours to set. Expiry dates are tracked where the goods need them.
Only the modules you use
Switch off what the shop does not need and the screen stays small. Everything else is one switch away in Settings.
Staff and roles
Cashiers see the till and their own receipts. The owner sees everything. Custom roles give access module by module.
Counter and phone
The till is built for a PC or laptop with a keyboard and a USB barcode scanner. The owner's pages, such as who owes what and what is running low, work on a phone.
Your data
Reports export to CSV, and backup and restore are built in. The shop owns its data.

Before you start

Anything else: {{ support_email }}

Does every plan start with the free days?

Yes. Each plan starts with {{ trial_days }} free days. The owner unlocks them by creating the account, verifying the email and finishing the business setup. A paid plan can be chosen or changed later.

Who controls the workspace?

The owner account that signs up becomes the admin with full control. Cashier and staff logins are created by the owner and stay limited by role.

Is it only invoices, or real accounts?

Real accounts. Every sale, purchase, return, expense and payment posts to a double-entry ledger, so profit and loss, the balance sheet and party balances come from the same entries as the till.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Reports export to CSV, and the shop owns its data.

Is there an edition that runs without internet?

Yes. A Windows edition installs on the shop's own PC, runs with no internet and keeps the data in the shop. Write to {{ support_email }} to ask about it.

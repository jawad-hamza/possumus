How automatic tax works
If you switch to Category GST, each product line uses the category GST matrix, including category-specific cash and bank GST when those rates differ. If you keep Manual Cart Tax but enable Payment Method GST, cash and bank will instead use the business-level GST rates from this page and the receipt will show both totals.

Which option should I use?

Stock Adjustment: when you already know how many units to add or remove, like damaged, lost, or opening balance fixes.

Stock Take: when staff counted the shelf and you want the system stock to become that final counted quantity.

Positive adjustments increase stock. Negative adjustments decrease stock. Stock take sets the final stock count exactly as counted.
